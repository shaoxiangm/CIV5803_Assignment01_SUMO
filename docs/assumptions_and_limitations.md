# Assumptions and limitations

1. Reference microscopic outputs were unavailable.
2. Demand was reconstructed from the supplied traffic evidence.
3. A complete 51-zone-to-SUMO connector mapping was unavailable.
4. A reduced, capacity-constrained demand (3,200 analysis vehicles plus warm-up) was used.
5. Observed queue and delay ground truth was unavailable for direct validation.
6. The OSM-derived network differs locally from the survey diagrams.
7. Queue, delay, speed and trajectory outputs are model-derived.
8. Results support comparative corridor assessment, not exact citywide prediction.

The baseline is frozen because insertion exceeded the assignment sufficiency threshold and the outputs distinguish corridor performance.
